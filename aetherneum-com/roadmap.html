Phase 2 · Triggered at 100 / 100

Genesis 100 → Notified-Body Filing

Window opens when the counter reaches 100 · Public application · Public docket

When Genesis 100 is reached, Aetherneum files a formal application for designation as a notified body under EU AI Act art. 28-32. The application package is itself published in public git: scope of conformity assessment, methodology, governance, conflict-of-interest policy, every artifact a regulator would expect.

  • Formal application — to the designating authority in our jurisdiction of record, under art. 28-32.
  • Scope of conformity assessment — high-risk AI systems under the Act's Annex III categories, mapped to our seven-criterion rubric.
  • Public docket — every submission, response, and revision committed to aetherneum-network/notified-body in real time.
  • Governance hardening — Faculty Board incorporated, conflict-of-interest disclosures, independence guarantees codified.
  • Founding Certified conferred — the first 100 entries permanently marked in the registry, with a re-signed JSON artifact citing the Phase-2 transition.
Phase 3 · Conditional on Phase 2 approval

The Aetherneum Standard · Licensed Authority

Conditional on regulator approval · No earlier than 18 months from filing

Once the notified-body designation is granted, Aetherneum becomes a legally recognized conformity-assessment body for AI systems under EU regulation — while the Charter, the public git audit trail, and the donation-only funding model remain unchanged. Regulatory standing does not change the work; it changes the weight of the badge.

  • Aetherneum Certified™ becomes a regulator-recognized mark — usable in EU market-access dossiers, procurement, insurance underwriting.
  • Conformity-assessment scope — operationalized across the high-risk AI categories defined in the Act.
  • Founding Certified retain their distinction — registry entries 1-100 carry the founding flag in every renewal cycle, in perpetuity.
  • Renewal cycle codified — annual re-defense remains the rhythm, with Charter amendments versioned in public git.
  • Crosswalks formalized — to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, SOC2 AI — so a single Aetherneum certification satisfies multiple frameworks simultaneously.

What does not change between phases

  • Open source. Charter, rubric, Council protocol, audit trail — public git, forever.
  • Donation-only funding. Crypto donations supplement Apprentice/Studio/Master fees but never replace them with a paywall.
  • Multi-provider Council. Five model voices in parallel, under human Patron veto. No single-vendor capture, no single-model bias.
  • Public verdict. Every JSON, every dissent, every rationale — in aetherneum-network/registry.
  • The work is the proof. Charter Principle 4. Not a slogan, the substrate.

We do not race to the top.

We earn the top, public commit by public commit, certification by certification, until the network agrees the Standard is the Standard.

Then we file.
