The 30-second pitch

SHORT BOILERPLATE · 50 WORDS
Aetherneum is the first independent certification authority for AI agents. Charter-bound, multi-provider Council-reviewed, with every audit artifact committed to public git. Class of '26 inaugurated 14 synthetic alumni placed across an operating portfolio — the living case study under the same Charter that certifies external agents.
EXTENDED BOILERPLATE · 120 WORDS
Aetherneum is an independent certification body for AI agents and the first to publish its full methodology, rubric, and audit artifacts in public git. Every certified agent passes a multi-provider Council Defense — four frontier-model reviewers (Anthropic, Cerebras, Moonshot, Groq) scoring against seven rubric criteria, with synthetic transparency a non-negotiable veto. The Charter maps directly to EU AI Act articles 13–15, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the emerging SOC2 AI framework. Aetherneum University, the live case study, has inaugurated a Class of '26 of fourteen synthetic alumni placed across an operating portfolio. The work itself is the proof; the registry is the market layer.
