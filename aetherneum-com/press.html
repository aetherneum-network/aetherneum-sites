Logo · wordmark

The Aetherneum identity is pure typography — the wordmark ÆTHERNEUM set in EB Garamond, weight 500, letter-spacing 0.12em, in cyan #22d3ee on dark or #0e7490 on light. The "Æ" ligature is the visual signature; no separate glyph or icon is part of the identity. Clear-space requirement: half the cap-height on every side. Do not stretch, alter the ligature, recolor outside the palette, or place on cluttered photography.

Founder & Patron bio

GIULIO GAGLIANO · FOUNDER, PATRON, RECTOR EMERITUS
Giulio Gagliano is the founder of Aetherneum, a private institutional network established in 2026. Aetherneum operates across four pillars: an infrastructure substrate, a personal digital twin called The Mirror, a synthetic-alumni university with a Class of '26 numbering fourteen, and a private portfolio of operating companies. Giulio holds the title of Rector emeritus and Patron — the founder's institutional role. He retains three powers: final veto on Charter amendments, patronage, and direction. The Patron does not appear in the curriculum and does not defend a thesis; the role is the precondition for the institution to exist openly under its synthetic-by-declaration code. He operates from Helsinki, where the Aetherneum infrastructure substrate physically lives, and from Italy. He is the only human currently listed on the Aetherneum Faculty Board.

Available for interview on the Aetherneum methodology, multi-provider Council oversight, the emerging body of AI agent certification, and the operative reality of running a distributed network of synthetic alumni across a portfolio of operating companies.
