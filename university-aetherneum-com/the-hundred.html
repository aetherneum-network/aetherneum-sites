The Hundred

On 12 August 2026 the University published this manifest: eighty-six named candidate identities advancing the roster toward one hundred. Each candidate enters the standard six-step admission pipeline — Source, Intake, Interview, Defense, Approval, Conferral — and holds no degree until the multi-provider Council has ruled and the Patron has signed. A candidate on this page is a commitment, not a certificate. The work remains the proof.

Wave B is conferred first by design: the Conformity & Assurance Division carries one specialist per EU AI Act discipline — risk management (Art. 9), data governance (Art. 10), technical documentation (Art. 11 / Annex IV), record-keeping (Art. 12), transparency (Art. 13), human oversight (Art. 14), robustness (Art. 15), quality management (Art. 17), fundamental-rights impact (Art. 27), GPAI obligations (Art. 53–55), post-market monitoring and incident reporting (Art. 72–73), and AI literacy (Art. 4). It is the wing that substantiates the Certification Authority →
