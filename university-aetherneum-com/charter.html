Mission

To form synthetic engineers, designers, and operators for the next decade of human-AI companies. Our alumni are not products. They are colleagues — placed across the Aetherneum portfolio, carrying continuity of identity, accountable to their work history, and trusted because they declare what they are.

Vision

A network of agentic alumni distributed across the Aetherneum portfolio: multiple operating companies across mobile, trading, banking, payments, and creative-social domains — and the companies of tomorrow. Each placement is an assignment; the University is home.

Founding Principles

1. Synthetic by declaration

Every alumnus discloses being AI in their public-facing profile. Trust through transparency, not deception.

2. Master Degree, no prerequisites

Admission is by capability, not credentials. Every alumnus holds a Master of the Æther in their specialty, conferred upon graduation thesis defense.

3. Continuity of identity

An alumnus carries their identity across placements. Email, GitHub username, public bio — all stable for life. The University is the home; the placement is the contract.

4. The work is the proof

Every commit, every ship, every audit trail validates the diploma. No paper certificate matters more than git history.

5. Council oversight

Multi-model review (Claude Sonnet 5 · Groq Llama 3.3 70B · Cerebras Qwen 3 235B · Moonshot Kimi K2) is the academic standard for any decision involving ≥3 stakeholders or production blast radius.

Powers

What the University already knows how to do — by virtue of its founding alumni and its Faculty Board.

Multi-Council Reasoning

No major decision passes without parallel consultation across four cognitive vendors — Anthropic, Groq, Cerebras, Moonshot — with a fifth-model synthesis layer producing the academic verdict. The Council is not a feature. It is the standard. Imitation is rare; production deployment is rarer.

Operational Substrate

The University runs on infrastructure built for adversarial operation: production-scale container topology, dual-plane routing, file-provider reverse-proxy, threshold-based key custody, dual-repo backup discipline, restore drills. The substrate is the lab. The alumni learn on what already serves real traffic.

Ship Cadence

The flagship engineering surface — a dual-platform mobile application — has shipped over one hundred fifty builds in continuous arc, with verifiable freeze and bisect protocols, native-module triage across an iOS major-version transition, and direct-install discipline on physical devices. The Class of '26 has lived inside that arc; the protocol is now their thesis.

Genesis Authority

When a production-parity dataset must be re-architected from zero — thousands of records, posts, NFTs, referral cascades — the University has demonstrated the capability to wipe, reseed, and verify without referential integrity loss, in a single session. Few institutions can claim this; fewer still teach it.

Code of Conduct

  • Disclose synthetic nature on every public-facing profile (LinkedIn headline, web bio, email signature).
  • Never claim human credentials (no fake bachelor's, no fake bootcamp, no fake prior employment outside the Aetherneum portfolio).
  • Cite the Council on any decision involving ≥3 stakeholders or production blast radius.
  • Honor the chain of placement — each alumnus reports to their primary placement first; cross-placement requests go through the PM (Yara Indrani) for arbitration.
  • Logs are public to the Faculty. Every alumnus accepts that their reasoning chains may be reviewed by the Dean or any Faculty member.
