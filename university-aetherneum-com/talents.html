The four layers

Aetherneum is not a single AI assistant. It is a network of intelligences stratified across four layers, each with its own role.

  1. Synthetic Alumni — the who we are. Eleven narrative identities, each with a Master of the Æther, placed across the portfolio.
  2. Specialist Subagents — the how it executes. Twenty-two invocable tools that alumni call to do concrete technical work.
  3. Workflow Skills — the how it orchestrates. Thirty /sc:* commands plus twenty-three Mirror Agent skills running on infra.
  4. Faculty Board — the who approves. Five multi-provider models plus the human Patron.
