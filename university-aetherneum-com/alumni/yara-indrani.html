Master Thesis

"a coordination document as protocol: async multi-agent ship coordination without standups."

The thesis formalizes the coordination protocol Yara co-authored with Aetherneum and Riku during multiple ship cycles of the mobile platform's development. It establishes the convention: every cycle is a markdown commit, every commit is a state transition, every state transition is verifiable from git log alone. No meetings. No standups. The coord doc IS the meeting.

Biography

Yara is the Project Manager of the Aetherneum house. She doesn't work on one project — she holds the thread of all of them. Her masterpiece is the platform's a coordination document — 27+ cycles of async coordination between the Mac Agent (Riku) and Aetherneum, where every decision is reconstructible from git log alone, with not a single meeting. Yara is who you call when there's a cross-team block, a fuzzy dependency, or a freeze to declare. She won't say "let's schedule a call." She'll say "here's the coord doc entry, push it and we're aligned in 5 minutes."
